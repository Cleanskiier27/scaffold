### Project Summary
LunaRecycle-OS resolves the critical challenge of non-metabolic solid waste accumulation during long-duration Artemis lunar surface missions. By combining twin-screw positive-displacement melt extrusion ($0.166\text{g}$ microgravity adapted) with continuous vacuum degassing and auxiliary in-situ regolith sintering, the system achieves:
- **Mass Recovery Yield:** $\mathbf{80.2\% - 95.0\%}$ conversion of packaging polymers, crew textiles, and structural foams.
- **Specific Energy Consumption:** $\mathbf{0.95 - 1.44\text{ kWh/kg}}$ (well within the $< 1.5\text{ kWh/kg}$ challenge threshold).
- **Mission Value:** Over **$1.13M USD** in equivalent launch payload mass savings per 30-day Artemis surface sortie ($50k/kg launch baseline).
- **Manufactured Assets:** Continuous 1.75mm FDM 3D printing filament, interlocking lunar habitat radiation shielding bricks, syngas, and potable ECLSS water.
