# NASA Aerospace Resume & Federal Application Guide

A comprehensive guide for engineers, researchers, and students applying for NASA Centennial Challenges, NASA Civil Service roles via USAJOBS, and University of Alabama aerospace research appointments.

---

## 1. Federal & Aerospace Resume Architecture

Unlike corporate 1-page resumes, federal and NASA engineering resumes are typically **2 to 4 pages long** and prioritize technical depth, compliance evidence, and quantifiable outcomes.

### Core Sections Required:
1. **Header & Eligibility**: Full Name, Location, Phone, Professional Email, U.S. Citizenship, Clearance Eligibility (Secret/TS/Public Trust).
2. **Professional Objective & Summary**: Clear articulation of target role, mission domain (e.g., Artemis ISRU, ECLSS, Systems Engineering), and core value proposition.
3. **Education & Academic Research**: Degree, Major, GPA (if $\ge 3.5$), University, Thesis/Capstone topics, relevant coursework.
4. **Technical Competencies & Space Domain Skills**: Categorized list of software, lab instrumentation, standards, and materials.
5. **Professional & Research Experience**: STAR/CCAR formatted experience entries detailing specialized engineering accomplishments.
6. **Publications, Patents & Awards**: Peer-reviewed conference papers (AIAA, IEEE, ASCEND), grants, NASA certificates.

---

## 2. The NASA STAR / CCAR Formatting Formula

Every bullet point should follow the **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) or **CCAR** (Context, Challenge, Action, Result) model with hard numbers:

$$\text{Action Verb} + \text{Technical Tool/Standard} + \text{Specific Task} + \text{Quantifiable Metric}$$

### Example Transformations:
- ❌ *Weak:* "Worked on recycling plastic for the moon challenge."
- ✅ *NASA High-Impact:* "Architected closed-loop solid waste recycling digital twin for NASA LunaRecycle Challenge, modeling polymer depolymerization and 0.166g melt extrusion to achieve 92.4% mass yield at <1.1 kWh/kg."

- ❌ *Weak:* "Did thermal analysis on equipment."
- ✅ *NASA High-Impact:* "Executed thermal-vacuum finite element analyses simulating extreme lunar surface diurnal cycles (-130°C to +120°C), eliminating thermal runaway risks in high-temperature polymer extrusion barrels."

## 3. NASA Competency Framework for LunaRecycle

The LunaRecycle-OS Scanner evaluates candidates against 5 key aerospace dimensions:

1. **In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) & Waste Processing**: Pyrolysis, extrusion, sintering, polymer degradation, ECLSS, circular economy.
2. **Lunar Surface & Extreme Environment Engineering**: Vacuum ($10^{-10}\text{ Pa}$), 0.166g gravity, dust mitigation, thermal radiation.
3. **NASA Systems Engineering & Standards**: NASA SP-2016-6105, ConOps, SWaP-C trade studies, FMEA, TRL advancement.
4. **Digital Twin & Computational Modeling**: FEA, CFD, mass/energy balance, sensor telemetry, anomaly detection.
5. **Aerospace Leadership & Allied Collaboration**: University of Alabama partnership, Centennial Challenges, grant management.
